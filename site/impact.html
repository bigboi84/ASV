Community impact

Measuring what matters.

Development-stage targets — subject to financing & program launch

AFSVHCL™ is committed to measurable, transparent community impact — from neurodivergent inclusion to youth athlete development, education and global diaspora empowerment.

Join the communityPartner with us

Impact framework

Six pillars of impact.

Neurodivergent Inclusion

Assessments, tutoring, employment readiness, assistive technology, family support and scholarships — funded by every membership.

Youth Athlete Development

Year-round multi-sport training, sports science and performance development for youth athletes across Canada.

Education & Mentorship

Academic support, mentorship and life skills integrated with athletic training through MLMSR Mentorship LLC.

Global Diaspora Community

Connecting Canada's multicultural communities worldwide to support youth development and community empowerment.

Community Partnerships

Public-private partnerships with municipalities, post-secondary institutions, school boards and corporate sponsors.

Transparent Reporting

Annual Impact Reports measuring and publishing real outcomes — not just aspirations.

Transparency & accountability

Annual impact reporting.

AFSVHCL is committed to publishing an Annual Impact Report — measuring and disclosing the real-world outcomes of the Neurodivergent Inclusion Fund, community programs and ecosystem partnerships. Impact is not a marketing claim. It is a commitment.
