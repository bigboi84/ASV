Neurodiversity · Education partnerships

Partner with us in education.

For schools, school boards, colleges, universities and learning organisations who want to help neurodivergent students thrive — in the classroom, on the field and beyond.

Become an education partner

Who this is for

Education partners only.

This page is for education partnerships. For sponsorship, corporate or community partnerships, visit Partners & Sponsors.

Schools & school boards

Elementary and secondary schools, boards and district programs.

Colleges & universities

Faculties of education, kinesiology, psychology and student services.

Tutoring & learning centres

Academic support providers working with neurodivergent learners.

Specialists

Educational psychologists, learning strategists and occupational therapists.

Education non-profits

Organisations focused on inclusion, literacy and student success.

Learning technology

Assistive and ed-tech providers that support different ways of learning.

How we could work together

Sport, learning and support — joined up.

Inclusive sport & PE

Structured, sensory-aware sport sessions that build confidence and social skills.

Tutoring & academic support

Homework help, literacy and numeracy support alongside training.

Teacher & coach training

Shared training on neurodiversity-affirming practice for staff and coaches.

Placements & internships

Student placements and work experience inside the village.

Research & outcomes

Measuring what works and sharing results with families and schools.

Family resources

Parent workshops and resources in Canada and the Caribbean.

The process

From first message to first program.

  1. 01

    Tell us about you

    Share your organisation, the students you serve and what you’d like to explore.

  2. 02

    Intro call

    Our education team will reach out within five business days to talk it through.

  3. 03

    Shape a pilot

    Together we design a small pilot that fits your students, timetable and goals.

  4. 04

    Agree & launch

    We put a simple agreement in place and launch as programs open.
