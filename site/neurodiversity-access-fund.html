Neurodiversity Access Fund

Helping Remove the Cost Barrier

AFSV VRC intends to develop a funding mechanism helping eligible individuals and families access selected programs and services regardless of financial circumstances.

Express Funding InterestSponsor an OutcomeRequest Partnership Information

AFSV VRC is not a charity. No donation receipt, tax deductibility, eligibility, funding availability or free service is promised. Contribution and payment functionality remains disabled until legal structure, accounting, privacy, eligibility and fund-administration rules are approved. The form below is an expression of interest, not a donation.

How the mechanism is intended to work.

01

Configurable outcomes

Sponsored outcomes and their values are administrator-configurable. Nothing is hardcoded and nothing is published before approval.

02

No payment route

There is no Donate button and no payment processing anywhere on this page. Expressions of interest are handled by people, not a gateway.

03

Eligibility later

Eligibility thresholds, award schedules and application windows follow legal, accounting and privacy sign-off.

04

Reported in aggregate

Awards are recorded for governance and reported in aggregate. No identifying participant information is published.
