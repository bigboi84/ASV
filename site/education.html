Education & Academic Success

Learning That Builds Confidence and Capability

AFSV VRC is developing academic support intended to help young people develop academically as well as physically and socially.

Register Learning InterestBecome an Education PartnerEducator / Tutor Inquiry

No tutoring service, diagnostic assessment, regulated support, named provider, curriculum approval or school partnership is live. Nothing here should be read as an available service.

How support is intended to work.

01

Alongside training

Study time sits inside the program schedule rather than competing with it, so participants are not choosing between the two.

02

Shared reporting

Where a school partnership is in place, coaches and educators work from the same progress information — with consent.

03

No clinical claims

Academic coaching is not assessment, diagnosis or regulated therapeutic support, and is never described as such.

04

Data minimisation

Public forms ask for the least information needed. Anything sensitive moves to an approved secure process later.

MLMSR Mentorship LLC

Proposed education & support programs.

Through MLMSR Mentorship LLC, AFSVHCL™ integrates academic support, mentorship and neurodivergent programming into every aspect of the proposed village.

Autism Spectrum Support

Structured programs integrating physical activity with social skills development and sensory-friendly environments.

ADHD Strategies

Focused training environments and mentorship tailored for athletes with ADHD — channelling energy into achievement and growth.

Dyslexia & Learning Support

Academic support and alternative learning methods for athletes with reading challenges and learning differences.

Family Support Resources

Resources and counselling for families navigating neurodivergent challenges — supporting the whole family.

Mentorship & Leadership

One-on-one and group mentorship connecting youth athletes with community leaders, coaches and role models.

Academic Integration

Proposed classroom and tutoring spaces within the dome — academic success alongside athletic development.
