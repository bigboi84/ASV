Accessibility, Privacy & Safeguarding

Access, Privacy and Safety by Design

What we commit to on accessibility, privacy and safeguarding, and how it is implemented. This is a commitment statement — the final legal policies are pending authorized approval.

Request Accessibility SupportReport an Accessibility IssuePrivacy Inquiry

Publication state: this page carries a commitment statement. The full Privacy Policy and Accessibility Statement require counsel and authorized approval before publication, and are listed as pending on the Legal and Policy page. General forms never ask for diagnoses or medical records.

01

Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA across the site.

  • Keyboard navigation
  • Screen-reader compatibility
  • Alt text
  • Captioned video and transcripts
  • Tested contrast
  • Readable typography
  • Clear navigation
  • Descriptive buttons
  • Accessible forms
  • Logical headings
  • Reduced flashing and motion
  • Mobile accessibility
  • Plain language and clear errors
02

Privacy

Collect only what is necessary, and say why.

  • Collect only necessary data
  • Clear notice and purpose
  • Explicit communications consent
  • Role-based access
  • Secure storage and transmission
  • Retention and deletion rules
  • Breach-response process
  • Approved analytics and cookie controls
03

Safeguarding

For children and vulnerable individuals.

  • Consent and guardian controls
  • Staff and provider screening requirements
  • Reporting and escalation route
  • Photography and testimonial authorization
  • Restricted data access
