Marketplace · Become a vendor

Sell with AFSV VRC.

We’re inviting brands, makers and community businesses to sell alongside the AFSV VRC collection. Tell us about your products and our team will review your application.

Apply to sell

The process

How becoming a vendor works.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Fill in the form below with your company details, how many products you’d like to sell and their value.

  2. 02

    Evaluation

    Your application is emailed to our marketplace team for review. We look at product fit, quality, values and pricing — usually within ten business days.

  3. 03

    Samples & terms

    Shortlisted vendors share samples and we agree simple, clear terms together.

  4. 04

    Go live

    We set up your listings, fulfilment and payments, ready for when the marketplace opens.

What we look for

Products our community will love.

Quality

Well-made products that last and that you’re proud of.

Shared values

Brands that support youth, sport, education and inclusion.

Reliable fulfilment

Stock you can deliver on time, every time.

Local & diaspora makers

Canadian, Caribbean and diaspora-owned businesses are very welcome.
