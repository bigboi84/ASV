Life Skills

Skills for Life Beyond Sport

Life-skills programming is intended to prepare participants for independence, leadership, careers and entrepreneurship.

Register InterestBecome a Mentor or Delivery PartnerSponsor Life-Skills Programming

All facilitators, curricula, safeguarding requirements, outcomes, dates and fees remain pending approval. No sessions are scheduled and none should be presented as available.

Program themes.

Thirteen themes, delivered in short blocks rather than as a single course.

01Communication
02Personal organization
03Time management
04Financial literacy and budgeting
05Transportation
06Workplace readiness
07Self-advocacy
08Social interaction
09Digital safety
10Personal responsibility
11Goal setting
12Leadership
13Entrepreneurship

Delivery concept.

01

Workshops

Short facilitated sessions on a single theme, sized to fit inside an existing program block. Subject to approved facilitators and schedules.

02

Mentoring

One-to-one and small-group mentoring, with mentors screened before any participant contact.

03

Integrated modules

Themes built into athlete-development sessions so the learning is not separated from the training.

04

Family resources

Material families can use at home, written in plain language.

05

Partner-led learning

Sessions delivered by employer, education and community partners under the same safeguarding requirements.

Register interest.

For participants, families, and anyone offering to mentor or deliver.

Safeguarding screening applies to every mentor and delivery partner before contact with participants.
