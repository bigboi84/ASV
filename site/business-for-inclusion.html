Business for Inclusion

Your Business Can Help Remove Barriers

AFSV VRC is developing a network of businesses committed to creating opportunities for neurodivergent individuals and families.

Become a Corporate PartnerSponsor a ProgramBecome a Neuroinclusive EmployerContribute Services

Sponsorship values and packages are administrator-configurable and are not published. Submissions feed an internal review workflow and are never auto-published.

Participation pathways.

Seven ways a business can take part.

01Funding Partner
02Service Partner
03In-Kind Partner
04Employment Partner
05Commercial Partner
06Program Sponsor
07Accessibility Partner

What a partnership involves.

01

Employer readiness

A review of recruitment practice, interview format and workplace environment before any candidate is introduced.

02

Role design

Roles written around the work that needs doing and the strengths of the candidate, not a generic job description.

03

Configurable contribution

Values and packages are set by an administrator, so nothing is hardcoded and nothing is published before approval.

04

Reviewed, not listed

A submission starts a review. It does not create a public listing, a logo placement or an announced partnership.
