Partners, sponsors & investors

Build the future with AFSV VRC.

AFSV VRC’s model depends on aligned partners across municipalities, education, sport, inclusion, technology, corporate sponsorship, community development and capital.

01

Why partner

AFSV VRC’s model depends on aligned partners across municipalities, education, sport, inclusion, technology, corporate sponsorship, community development and capital.

02

Partnership categories

Six audience routes cover sponsorship, education and community, technology, service and in-kind contribution, development and infrastructure, and investor inquiry.

03

Outcome sponsorship

Sponsorship structured around measurable participation, learning and inclusion outcomes. Values and packages are administrator-configurable and not published until approved.

04

Development partnerships

Site, design, construction, technology and operating partnerships connected to the proposed Whitby pilot.
