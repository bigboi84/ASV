GAISB AI Education & Responsible Innovation

Advancing Responsible AI Education and Workforce Readiness

Through its strategic relationship with the Global AI Standards Body, AFSV VRC is advancing AI education, certification, workforce readiness and responsible innovation across sport, education and community development in Canada and the Caribbean.

Explore Certification InterestBecome an Education PartnerDiscuss Enterprise AISummit & Events

This is a strategic relationship with the Global AI Standards Body. No accreditation, exclusivity or course availability is claimed beyond approved language, and no GAISB logo or brand mark is published pending written permission. Programs, territories, certifications, fees and dates appear only once contractually approved.

Priority pathways.

Eight pathways within the confirmed scope of the relationship.

01AI literacy
02Professional certification
03Educator and youth learning
04Sport technology
05Responsible-AI governance
06Workforce readiness
07Enterprise innovation
08Community capacity building

AI supports the pillars. It does not replace them.

01

Athlete development

Analysis and planning tools support coaching decisions. The coach makes the decision.

02

Inclusive learning

Assistive and adaptive tools widen access to material. They do not substitute for a teacher or a support worker.

03

Neurodivergent support

Tools chosen for accessibility and predictability, reviewed with the people who use them.

04

Operations

Scheduling, reporting and administration, so staff time goes to participants rather than paperwork.

05

Media and career preparation

Production and career-readiness skills that reflect how the work is actually done now.
