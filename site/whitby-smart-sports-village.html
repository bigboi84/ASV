Proposed development

The Whitby Smart Sports Village: a connected place to train, learn and belong.

AFSV VRC is advancing a proposed Smart Sports Village pilot in Whitby, Ontario: a year-round, technology-enabled destination bringing multi-sport participation, athlete development, education, inclusive supports, media and community experiences under one roof.

Concept at a glance.

ScaleProposed

Approximately 150,000 sq ft in proposed Phase 1, subject to site, design, approvals and financing.

Sport

Flexible soccer, cricket, basketball, sprint and configurable sport space.

Performance

Performance, strength and conditioning, recovery, biomechanics and analytics.

Learning

Learning, tutoring, mentoring, sensory support and life skills.

Media

Esports, broadcasting, podcasting and content creation.

Community

Events, partner activation, concessions, retail and fan experiences.

Project overview

The foundation for a national network.

The Smart Sports Village is the proposed flagship pilot of AFSVHCL™ — designed as the model for a national network of integrated sports and education campuses across Canada.

The proposed dome would integrate elite athletic development, applied learning, neurodivergent support pathways, esports and digital media, and measurable community impact — all under one roof, year-round. It is proposed for a 12–15 acre Smart Sports & Education Campus in partnership with municipal and post-secondary institutions, subject to financing, approvals and feasibility analysis.
