EFN · Esports & Fans Network

Where sports meets digital.

EFN is the proposed digital and esports division of AFSVHCL™ — connecting athletes and fans through competitive gaming, live streaming and interactive media. Home of FanZone™.

Get connectedExplore FanZone™

The network

Four ways EFN connects.

01

Esports tournaments

Competitive gaming events for amateur and professional players.

02

Live streaming events

Broadcast-quality production for sports and esports content.

03

Athlete media content

Podcast studio, interviews and athlete storytelling.

04

Global fan engagement

Digital memberships, live stats and interactive experiences.

EFN platform features

The digital ecosystem.

A world-class esports arena, broadcasting studio and digital media hub — all integrated into the sports village ecosystem.

Esports Arena

80–120 gaming stations, tournament stage and streaming control centre integrated into the Smart Sports Village.

Live Streaming & Broadcasting

Broadcast-quality production for sports competitions, esports tournaments and athlete media content.

Sports Village Podcast Network

Podcast studio and storytelling platform amplifying athletes, coaches and community leaders.

Athlete Data & Analytics

A digital athlete management platform tracking performance, development and recruitment data.

Fan Membership Platform

Digital memberships, live stats, interactive fan experiences and community engagement tools.

Esports Leagues & Tournaments

Competitive events for amateur and professional players across multiple game titles and platforms.
