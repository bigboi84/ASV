Neurodiversity Access & Opportunity

Different Minds. Equal Opportunity.

AFSV VRC is developing an inclusive ecosystem intended to expand access to sport, education, developmental support, life skills, technology and employment for neurodivergent people. Through qualified professionals, community organizations, businesses, sponsors and employers, the program intends to reduce financial and accessibility barriers.

Get Support / Register InterestBecome a Service ProviderBecome a Neuroinclusive EmployerSponsor Access

AFSV VRC does not provide clinical diagnosis, assessment or therapeutic treatment. Regulated services are delivered only by qualified professionals who are reviewed separately, and the public form does not ask for diagnoses or medical records.
