Programs & Athlete Development

Develop the Athlete. Strengthen the Whole Person.

AFSV VRC is developing inclusive pathways intended to support participation, skill development, performance, confidence and opportunity.

What each program record holds.

01

Structured records

Approved title, status, age group, ability level, format, schedule, location, capacity, prerequisites, accessibility, coach or provider, price, media and registration route.

02

Status labels

Every program carries a visible status. Until it is approved the record shows “Program details coming soon” and collects interest only.

03

Adaptive pathway

Adaptive and sensory-friendly sport is a named category in its own right, not an accommodation applied to another program.

04

Registration later

Registration and payment stay switched off until inventory, schedules, pricing, waivers and refund terms are approved.
