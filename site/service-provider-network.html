Service Provider Network

Become an AFSV VRC Service Provider

AFSV VRC intends to build a qualified network supporting sport, learning, development, wellbeing and inclusion.

Register Provider Interest

Submission does not approve or publicly list a provider. All providers remain subject to qualification, licence and registration, insurance, safeguarding, privacy, capacity and agreement reviews.

Potential categories.

Ten categories under consideration. A category being listed does not mean a provider is available.

01Occupational therapy
02Speech and language
03Behavioural services
04Counselling and mental health
05Executive function
06Academic intervention
07Family education
08Service navigation
09Social development
10Adaptive and inclusive sport

What review covers.

01

Qualification and registration

Regulated professions must evidence current licence or registration. Those fields are conditional and only appear where they apply.

02

Insurance and safeguarding

Professional indemnity, screening and safeguarding arrangements are checked before any participant contact.

03

Capacity and coverage

Age groups, specialisation, geography and delivery mode, so referrals are only made where a provider can genuinely take them.

04

Agreement and listing

Listing follows a signed agreement. Until then a submission sits in a review queue with an auditable status.

Register provider interest.

This is an application to be reviewed, not a listing request.

Licence and registration fields are conditional on regulated profession. Do not attach client records or identifying participant information.

Fields marked *with an asterisk are required.
